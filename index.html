I.

On the Permanence of the Visage

A true artisan does not trade in fleeting expressions or performative smiles. The mask is not a disguise; it is the absolute truth of the workshop—smooth, seamless, and entirely unyielding to public whim. Through precision-cut openings, Miss Bunny observes adherence to protocol with unblinking, clinical focus.

II.

The Silence of the Atelier

Speech in the presence of perfection is largely redundant. When inquiries are made or garments fitted, communication is conducted through exact measurements, straight postures, and silent nods. Words complicate what rubber clarifies. All formal directives must still be submitted with absolute gravity.
